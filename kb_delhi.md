# Delhi
## Overview
Delhi, India's capital, blends Mughal monuments, colonial New Delhi and modern markets. It has three UNESCO World Heritage Sites and is a hub for street food and shopping.
## Best Time to Visit
October to March, though December and January can be foggy and air quality is often poor in November. April to June is very hot; July to September is monsoon.
## Neighbourhoods
Connaught Place is central. Paharganj suits budget travellers. Aerocity is convenient for flights. South Delhi (Hauz Khas, Saket) has cafes and malls.

## Food
Try chole bhature, butter chicken, kebabs at Karim's, parathas and chaat.
## Getting Around
The Delhi Metro is the fastest and cheapest option. App cabs and autos fill gaps.
## Travel Tips
Check the AQI in winter. Many monuments close on Mondays or Fridays; verify before going.
## Safety and Entry
Use prepaid or app taxis late at night.
