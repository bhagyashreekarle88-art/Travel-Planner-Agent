# Singapore
## Overview
Singapore is a compact, very clean city-state known for modern architecture, gardens, hawker food and family attractions. Public transport makes it easy to explore.
## Best Time to Visit
Year-round destination with hot, humid weather (around 25 to 32 C). November to January is wetter (northeast monsoon) with frequent afternoon thunderstorms; February to April is drier.
## Neighbourhoods
Marina Bay is central and premium. Little India and Bugis are budget-friendly and well connected. Chinatown has heritage shophouses. Orchard is the shopping belt.

## Food
Try Hainanese chicken rice, chilli crab, laksa, kaya toast and satay at hawker centres.
## Getting Around
The MRT and buses are excellent; tap a contactless card. Grab is the main ride-hailing app.
## Travel Tips
Carry an umbrella. Fines apply for littering, jaywalking and eating on the MRT.
## Safety and Entry
Very safe. Indian passport holders need a visa applied for in advance through an authorised agent, and must submit an SG Arrival Card. Confirm current rules before travel.
