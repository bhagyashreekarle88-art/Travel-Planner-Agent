# Bangkok
## Overview
Bangkok, Thailand's capital, combines ornate temples, floating markets, riverside life, street food and nightlife. It is one of the most affordable international trips from India.
## Best Time to Visit
November to February is coolest and driest. March to May is very hot. May to October is the rainy season with short heavy downpours, usually in the afternoon.
## Neighbourhoods
Sukhumvit is central, well connected and good for nightlife. Silom and Riverside are near temples and the river. Siam is the shopping hub. Khao San Road suits backpackers.

## Food
Try pad thai, tom yum, green curry, mango sticky rice and boat noodles. Vegetarian options are available but confirm fish sauce use.
## Getting Around
The BTS Skytrain and MRT avoid traffic. Chao Phraya Express boats reach riverside temples. Grab and Bolt are common.
## Travel Tips
Cover shoulders and knees at temples. Avoid tuk-tuk drivers offering cheap tours with shop stops.
## Safety and Entry
Generally safe; beware of gem and tailor scams. Indian passport holders have had visa-exempt short stays, but rules have changed several times; confirm with the Royal Thai Embassy before travel.
