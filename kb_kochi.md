# Kochi
## Overview
Kochi (Cochin) is Kerala's port city and gateway to the backwaters, hill stations and beaches. Fort Kochi and Mattancherry preserve Portuguese, Dutch, British, Jewish and Arab influences, while Ernakulam is the modern commercial centre.
## Best Time to Visit
October to March is best with lower humidity. The southwest monsoon (June to September) is very wet but popular for Ayurveda. April and May are hot and humid.
## Neighbourhoods
Fort Kochi is ideal for heritage, cafes and homestays. Mattancherry is for markets and the Jew Town. Marine Drive and MG Road in Ernakulam are central and well connected.

## Food
Try appam with stew, Kerala sadya on a banana leaf, karimeen pollichathu (pearl spot fish) and Malabar parotta. Many spice shops sell cardamom and pepper.
## Getting Around
Ferries connect Ernakulam and Fort Kochi cheaply. Kochi Metro and app cabs serve the mainland. Rent a bicycle in Fort Kochi.
## Travel Tips
Carry an umbrella in monsoon months. Dress modestly at religious sites.
## Safety and Entry
Safe and tourist-friendly.
