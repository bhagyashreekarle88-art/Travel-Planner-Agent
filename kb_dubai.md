# Dubai
## Overview
Dubai, in the United Arab Emirates, is known for record-breaking architecture, luxury shopping, desert experiences and beaches. It is a short flight from most Indian cities.
## Best Time to Visit
November to March is ideal (20 to 30 C). June to September is extremely hot (often above 40 C), so outdoor activity is limited to early morning and evening; hotels are cheaper then.
## Neighbourhoods
Deira and Bur Dubai are older, cheaper and close to the souks. Downtown has the Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall. Dubai Marina and JBR suit beach lovers. Jumeirah has resorts.

## Food
Try shawarma, Emirati machboos and luqaimat, and Indian food in Karama and Bur Dubai.
## Getting Around
The Dubai Metro is clean and efficient; get a Nol card. Taxis and app cabs are plentiful.
## Travel Tips
Dress modestly in public areas and souks. Public displays of affection and drinking in public are prohibited.
## Safety and Entry
Very safe. Indian passport holders generally need a visa; some with valid US or UK visas or residence permits may qualify for visa on arrival. Always confirm current rules with official sources before travel.
