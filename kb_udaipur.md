# Udaipur
## Overview
Udaipur, the City of Lakes in southern Rajasthan, is known for marble palaces on lakes Pichola and Fateh Sagar, romantic sunsets, and Mewar heritage. It is a popular destination for couples and weddings.
## Best Time to Visit
September to March has pleasant weather; post-monsoon months have full lakes. April to June is hot. July and August bring monsoon greenery.
## Neighbourhoods
Lal Ghat and Gangaur Ghat near Lake Pichola are atmospheric with rooftop cafes. Fateh Sagar is quieter. Hiran Magri and Ambamata have newer, cheaper hotels.

## Food
Try dal baati, gatte ki sabzi, kachori and rooftop dining with lake views.
## Getting Around
The old city is walkable; use auto-rickshaws or app cabs for Sajjangarh and outlying sights.
## Travel Tips
Book sunset boat slots in advance in winter. Lanes are narrow and steep.
## Safety and Entry
Very safe for tourists; standard precautions apply.
