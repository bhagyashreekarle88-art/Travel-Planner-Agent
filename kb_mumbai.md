# Mumbai
## Overview
Mumbai is India's financial capital and home of Bollywood, with colonial-era architecture, seafronts, markets and an energetic food culture.
## Best Time to Visit
November to February is pleasant. The monsoon (June to September) brings very heavy rain and occasional flooding. March to May is hot and humid.
## Neighbourhoods
Colaba and Fort are best for heritage. Bandra has cafes and nightlife. Juhu is near the beach and airport. Lower Parel has business hotels and dining.

## Food
Try vada pav, pav bhaji, Irani cafe bun maska, bhel puri at Chowpatty and coastal seafood.
## Getting Around
Local trains are fast but crowded; the Metro, app cabs and black-yellow taxis are common.
## Travel Tips
Avoid local trains at peak hours with luggage. Monsoon flooding can disrupt travel.
## Safety and Entry
Generally safe; watch for pickpockets in crowds.
