# Jaipur
## Overview
Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan, is called the Pink City for its terracotta-painted old town. It is part of India's Golden Triangle with Delhi and Agra and is known for Rajput forts, palaces, bazaars, block-print textiles and gemstones.
## Best Time to Visit
October to March is ideal with sunny, mild days and cool nights. April to June is very hot (often above 40 C), so sightseeing should be early morning or evening. July to September brings monsoon showers.
## Neighbourhoods
The Old City (Pink City) is best for heritage and markets. C-Scheme and MI Road are central with good restaurants. Bani Park is quieter with heritage guesthouses. Amer Road is convenient for the forts.
## Attractions
- Amber Fort | outdoor | heritage, culture, family | 3 | 200 | morning | Amer | Hilltop fort-palace of red sandstone and marble with the Sheesh Mahal mirror hall.
- Hawa Mahal | outdoor | heritage, art | 1 | 50 | morning | Old City | Five-storey honeycomb facade with 953 small windows, best photographed in early light.
- City Palace | indoor | heritage, culture, art | 2 | 300 | afternoon | Old City | Royal residence with museums of textiles, arms and courtyards.
- Jantar Mantar | outdoor | heritage, culture | 1 | 50 | morning | Old City | UNESCO-listed 18th-century astronomical observatory with giant sundials.
- Albert Hall Museum | indoor | culture, art, heritage | 1.5 | 50 | afternoon | Ram Niwas Garden | Indo-Saracenic building housing the state museum, lit up beautifully at night.
- Nahargarh Fort Sunset | outdoor | heritage, nature | 2 | 50 | evening | Aravalli Hills | Fort on the ridge with panoramic sunset views over the city.
- Jaigarh Fort | outdoor | heritage | 1.5 | 100 | morning | Amer | Fort that holds the Jaivana, one of the world's largest wheeled cannons.
- Johari and Bapu Bazaar | outdoor | shopping, food | 2 | 0 | evening | Old City | Markets for jewellery, textiles, mojari shoes and street food.
- Chokhi Dhani Village Experience | outdoor | culture, food, family | 3 | 1100 | evening | Tonk Road | Rajasthani village-themed evening with folk dance, puppetry and a thali dinner.
- Patrika Gate | outdoor | art, culture | 0.5 | 0 | morning | Jawahar Circle | Vividly painted gateway, popular for photography.
- Panna Meena ka Kund | outdoor | heritage, art | 0.5 | 0 | morning | Amer | Symmetrical 16th-century stepwell near Amber Fort.
- Block Printing Workshop | indoor | art, culture, shopping | 2 | 800 | afternoon | Sanganer | Hands-on session in traditional hand block printing.
## Food
Try dal baati churma, laal maas, ghewar, pyaaz kachori and lassi. Rawat Mishthan Bhandar and Laxmi Mishthan Bhandar are well-known institutions.
## Getting Around
App cabs and auto-rickshaws are widely available. A composite ticket covers several monuments and saves money. Hire a car for a full day of forts.
## Travel Tips
Start forts early to avoid heat and crowds. Bargain in bazaars. Carry water and sun protection.
## Safety and Entry
Generally safe; beware of gem-scam touts promising resale profits.
