# Varanasi
## Overview
Varanasi (Banaras, Kashi), on the Ganges in Uttar Pradesh, is one of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities and the spiritual heart of Hinduism. Life centres on more than 80 ghats; nearby Sarnath is where the Buddha gave his first sermon.
## Best Time to Visit
October to March is best. Dev Deepawali (November) is spectacular. April to June is extremely hot. July to September brings floods that can submerge lower ghats and limit boat rides.
## Neighbourhoods
Assi Ghat is relaxed with cafes. Dashashwamedh and Godowlia are central and busy. Cantonment has larger hotels and quieter streets.

## Food
Try kachori-sabzi, tamatar chaat, malaiyo, lassi in clay cups and Banarasi paan.
## Getting Around
Lanes near the ghats are walkable but crowded; use e-rickshaws and boats. Cars cannot reach most ghats.
## Travel Tips
Photography at cremation ghats (Manikarnika) is disrespectful. Agree on boat fares in advance.
## Safety and Entry
Safe with normal precautions; watch belongings in crowds.
